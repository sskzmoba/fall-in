Dawn @ Kolukkumalai

Sunrise trek & tent stay · Munnar

  • 📅Sat–Sun, 5–6 December 2026
  • 🚌Departs 5:30 AM, 5 Dec · Vivekananda Park, Kowdiar, TVM
  • 💰~₹5,000 per person
    ₹3,600 if travelling direct (no bus) · ₹2,000 advance
  • ⏳Book by 31 Oct · Balance by 7 Nov

A night under the stars, a 4:00 AM wake-up — earlier than the mess siren! — and a sunrise from one of the highest tea estates in the world.

What’s included

Bus from TVM, jeep transfers, trekking, food and tea on both days. Final expenses are settled on actual costs. Spouse or immediate family may join a participating Kazak (add their details in the form).

Itinerary
  1. Day 1 · Sat 5 Dec
  2. 5:30 AMDepart Kowdiar, TVM
  3. En routeBreakfast & lunch
  4. 2:00 PMReach Suryanelli
  5. 4:00 PMJeep to campsite · tea
  6. 5:00 PMSunset viewpoint
  7. EveningCampfire, socialising, group activities · lights out 10:30 PM
  8. Day 2 · Sun 6 Dec
  9. 4:00 AMWake up
  10. DawnTrek to the sunrise viewpoint
  11. MorningBreakfast at camp · check-out 11:00 AM
  12. AfternoonMunnar sightseeing & shopping
  13. 10:00 PMExpected return to TVM

Schedule may change with weather and local conditions.

Booking notes
  • Slots are confirmed after payment verification, first come, first served.

The Forest Trail

Coimbatore · Sathyamangalam · Bandipur · Gudalur

Where the Western & Eastern Ghats meet: Bhavanisagar dam, Sathyamangalam’s forest villages and their stories, the 27 hairpins of Dhimbam Ghat, and elephant, gaur and deer country through Bandipur.

  • 📅23–25 January 2027
    +1 optional day on Republic Day, 26 Jan
  • 🧭Bangalore, Kochi & Calicut groups meet at Coimbatore · join en route
  • 💰~₹13,000–15,000 per person
    Stay, food & fuel · self-funded · ₹2,500 token advance
  • ⏳Booking open

After our first Kazak ride to the Nilgiris in 2026, the roads are calling again — on a route the riders chose themselves.

Who can join
  • Kazaks with long-distance riding experience, joining at Bangalore, Kochi, Calicut or en route.
  • One accompanying person (18+) as pillion or on another bike.
  • Full safety gear is a must.
  • Final costs and arrangements are shared in the riders’ group.
